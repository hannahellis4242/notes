\section{Introduction}

In this chapter we will introduce the perifocal coordinate system and 
derive the equations of motion in this coordinate system. We will also 
establish the angular momentum, and total energy. Finally we will show
that closed solutions to the equations of motion in the perifocal coordinate
system are ellipses.

As a bonus, we will also revisit the eccentricity vector, and show how it
relates to the solutions from this chapter.
